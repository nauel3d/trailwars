# November 10 controls

| Action | Keyboard | Controller | Phone/tablet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turn left/right | A/D or Left/Right; hold | D-pad or left thumbstick | Hold left/right buttons |
| Homing | W or Up | X | Homing button |
| Jump | S or Down | Y | Jump button |
| Post-elimination rocket | Same left/right controls | Same steering | Same steering buttons |

Release steering to fly straight. Losing focus releases steering; the server also expires stale steering. Each ability has its own server cooldown, displayed on its button. Jump crosses trails and cores but does **not** protect from walls. Homing seeks a nearby living opponent and steals three available points; a hit is not a kill.

After elimination, turrets automatically launch a separate rocket while the round remains active. Initially it aims toward an opponent; steer it with the same left/right controls. Only one rocket per owner may exist. A miss can occupy its five-second lifetime before another launch. A hit steals one available point and never resurrects the owner or awards a kill. Turn turrets off in host settings to spectate instead.

Home → Settings lets the queue host choose target score, round cap, overtime and turrets. Changes apply to the next series. Home → Profile shows authoritative current-server statistics. Intermission offers a local animation skip; it does not advance the server clock. Play Again joins a new series after final results.

Controller mappings and touch-button bounds are implemented; physical controller and physical phone testing remain outstanding. See checklist for the exact verified scope.
