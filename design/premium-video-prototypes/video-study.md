## Why the rejected proposals missed the target

They depended on simple vector silhouettes, largely flat material, thin body typography, quiet color and repeated geometric panels. The supplied video puts much more of its quality into custom illustration, layered text edging, tonal separation, highlights, physical control depth and meaningful product-specific art. A more saturated recolor of the same simple drawings would not close that gap.

The new prototypes explicitly change the illustration method and material treatment. Their finish is still a proposal requiring your judgment; native implementation fidelity, animation quality, controller/touch behavior and performance are not demonstrated by static images.

The reference's collection mechanics, pets/eggs, giveaway/premium-currency systems, branding and exact compositions are excluded. Trail Wars keeps original racers/trails and existing gameplay/statistics. We are evaluating UI craftsmanship, not reconstructing this reference game.
