# Visual reference study — 10 November 2026

Eight Roblox experiences, selected for distinct visual problems rather than popularity. Original images are stored in `references/`; attribution, image URLs, source dates, dimensions and SHA-256 are in `references/sources.json`. `inspection-sheet.jpg` was visually inspected. Dates describe the publication or post, not guaranteed UI version. Historical evidence is deliberately marked; it does not establish today's interface.

Evidence labels: **Observed** = visible in the saved image. **Judgment** = our design interpretation. **Linked** = footage located but moving playback/audio not inspected. No timings, pressed states or sound qualities are attributed to a game from still images. Public listings identify the right experience; they do not prove mobile ergonomics. No reference artwork is a proposed Trail Wars asset.

## 1. Block Tales — paper RPG

[Official experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/16483433878/Block-Tales) · [Battle screenshot, June 2024](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/block-tales-cards-tier-list) · [Demo 5 teaser, December 2025 — linked, not played](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2YgTTbc7qwI).

**Observed:** desaturated voxel world; compact textured action tray in the upper left; tiny illustrated cards; separate HP/SP plate near the character. Squared edges, restrained color and small outlined lettering belong to the same retro vocabulary. Menu and gameplay share a single battle image. No glossy button hierarchy is visible.

**Judgment:** substrate, type and imagery agreeing is more convincing than adding decorative borders. Adopt that consistency for B. Do not import its tiny text, RPG action menu or slow-paced composition into competitive steering. Mobile support is stated on the listing; mobile layout, pressed feedback, transitions and sound remain unverified here. No developer art dossier was found in this focused pass.

## 2. Tower Heroes — character-led collection

[Official experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/4646477729/Tower-Heroes) · [Developer studio](https://info.tower-heroes.com/) · [Collection, December 2023](https://thenerdstash.com/all-towers-in-tower-heroes-ranked-tier-list/) · [Gameplay, July 2024](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/tower-heroes-cake-factory-guide) · [Footage discovered through a video index — linked, not played](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DuZLyejIngA).

**Observed:** collection menu uses dark framing, white silhouettes, compact category tabs, repeated ownership labels and one large selected-character preview. Gameplay places wave/health information high and unit portraits low. The portraits contribute more identity than the fairly conventional rectangular panels. Typography prioritizes labels and numbers; outlines are economical rather than uniformly heavy.

**Judgment:** isolate one inspectable vehicle instead of rotating a grid of live 3D previews. Character diversity can carry collection charm. Reject its collection density for Home; don't mistake repeated cards for art direction. Motion, pressed depth, audio and physical mobile behavior are unverified. Developer website establishes authorship, not interaction quality.

## 3. PHIGHTING! — competitive character staging

[Official experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/7138009149/PHIGHTING) · [Selection screenshot post](https://x.com/2ThousandSunny/status/2034021283617288699) · [2022 gameplay footage — linked, not played](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cLBXYI88ZzY).

**Observed:** large, slanted white selection heading; character dominates the center; a narrow, unboxed role roster occupies the right edge. A restrained dark backdrop gives the red/black character a strong silhouette. Labels are arranged around the character rather than inside matching cards. A small difficulty indicator adds subordinate information.

**Judgment:** the strongest lesson is composition, not copying angled typography. Give Home/Results an expressive core with navigation secondary. The long narrow roster and all-caps detail would be fragile on phones. This capture supports selection analysis only: live-HUD readability, mobile layout, button press, animation and sound were not directly observed. The post is dated; the underlying game build is unknown.

## 4. Super Bomb Survival — controls amid chaos

[Official experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/164051105/Super-Bomb-Survival) · [Historical gameplay screenshot, 2022 forum thread](https://forum.arcaneodyssey.dev/t/super-bomb-survival-pics-here/82618) · [Developer update poster, 2019](https://x.com/tylermcbride/status/1094617549638127616) · [2026 gameplay footage — linked, not played](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ALQWr-FuUQ).

**Observed:** timer high, health low, expressive illustrated skill art at the lower corners. Strong reds/yellows and hazard labels compete with the action; the pictured vignette reduces peripheral clarity. The developer poster shows a world-integrated Survival Shop sign, not a working menu screenshot.

**Judgment:** ability silhouettes should be unmistakable and remain subordinate to the arena. Borrow distinct action icons; reject the density, strong vignette and center-crossing feedback. A 3D shop/lobby is inappropriate for Trail Wars. No observed menu pressed state, timing, mobile controls or sound. A real shop-menu capture is an evidence gap; the lobby poster is not substituted for one.

## 5. Bee Swarm Simulator — collectible icon vocabulary

[Official experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/1537690962/Bee-Swarm-Simulator) · [Official Roblox video poster, 2019](https://x.com/Roblox/status/1102628079325900800) · [Mobile test-realm footage, 2022 — linked, not played](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iHVZy-JM3AU).

**Observed:** a white inventory column with colorful object icons and descriptive text, a small monochrome navigation row, large numeric resource readouts and a dense item strip along the bottom. Green field and red/white world shapes frame this utilitarian interface. Most controls are flat; the recognizable vocabulary comes from objects and resource symbols.

**Judgment:** consistent original icons are valuable even without elaborate surfaces. Its number density and tiny peripheral icons are a poor match for six-racer competition. This is a counterexample to copying every successful game. The poster is still evidence only; no moving interaction or audio was reviewed. Mobile footage is a test realm, not a certification of current production responsiveness. No pressed-state claim.

## 6. Dandy's World — character context / quiet survival HUD

[Official experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/16116270224/Dandys-World) · [Shop, July 2024](https://thenerdstash.com/the-ultimate-beginners-guide-to-dandys-world-roblox/) · [Gameplay, August 2024](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/dandys-world-toon-tier-list).

**Observed:** shop counter character fills the left; a dark trinket tray sits right with a clearly separated EXIT. Gameplay is far quieter: narrow floor/objective band above, hearts/stamina below, broad center left to the world. White lettering and grayscale surfaces let character colors carry expression. The shop's repeated cells are inventory-specific, not the whole interface.

**Judgment:** use stronger character treatment outside active rounds and quiet essential information inside them. Reject horror darkness, tiny item labels and its specific mascot/room composition. Character art must be original. [Long-run footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vs-jV3AgB8k) was found, but its direct page fetch was throttled. Video interaction, sound, pressed states and device-specific responsiveness were not verified; no mobile or developer art source was confirmed in this pass.

## 7. Super Doomspire — toy combat / sticker collection

[Official experience and media](https://www.roblox.com/games/3725149043/Super-Doomspire) · [Developer concept sketch, shared 2022](https://x.com/tylermcbride/status/1557499803172573184) · [Listing-associated video — linked, not played](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pp4ZFiap05E).

**Observed:** selected item enlarged beside a grid, warm coral/yellow heading, category chips and illustrated tools. Tooltip text separates attributes from description. The developer sketch has labels, flags and an MVP statue idea; it is explicitly a concept, not shipped UI. Logo composition has much stronger visual weight than the collection frame.

**Judgment:** one understandable toy-object silhouette can unify icons, inspect art and reward tokens. B can borrow the principle of collectible sticker expression. Reject dense inventory during play and the exact tools, stickers and logo. Saved media supports inventory/art analysis; combat HUD, mobile controls, transition timings, press behavior and audio remain unverified. The API-served image retrieval date is known; its capture date is not.

## 8. Epic Minigames — party navigation / round reporting

[Official experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/277751860/Epic-Minigames) · [Menu, shop, achievement and winner-banner screenshots, May 2024](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/epic-minigames-guide) · [Footage discovered through a video index — linked, not played](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3oePOQvgRP4).

**Observed:** oversized green Quick Play is more prominent than Find/Create; Return is separately colored. Achievement menu uses yellow framing and repeated red cells. The round winner is a thin text banner above the environment. Small peripheral icons keep the middle free, but text and colors can compete with the world.

**Judgment:** a single dominant entry action is a strong transferable rule. Its plain rectangular buttons and dense reward grid are not the artistic benchmark for Trail Wars. Final placements deserve more expression than a top-line sentence. Menu depth, actual pressed state, animation, sound and mobile touch ergonomics are unverified. Use the dated screenshot as a composition study, not proof of today's UI.

## Synthesis and evidence limits

The consistent transferable principle is **specific character/object art + deliberate hierarchy + quieter active-play framing**. Eight games do not imply eight endorsements. Bee Swarm/Epic inventory density and conventional collection grids are explicit counterexamples.

Coverage is intentionally transparent: 13 saved, inspected images cover eight experiences, including menu, gameplay and developer-art examples across the set. We did not obtain every requested evidence type for every game. Moving playback/audio and physical-phone behavior are unverified; direct footage links are provided for all eight games, with two discovered through video indexes. Direct fetches of the Tower Heroes, Epic Minigames and Dandy clips were throttled; no moving-playback observations are claimed. The approval gallery includes direct discovered clips where available and marks gaps. The proposal's motion is original and testable, not falsely presented as observed reference behavior. No access restrictions were bypassed.
